Sunrise College of Engineering — Knowledge Base
===============================================

ADMISSIONS
Admissions for the 2026-27 batch open on 1st May 2026 and close on 30th June 2026.
Eligibility: 50% aggregate in 12th standard with Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics.
Admission is based on the CET entrance exam score followed by document verification.
Required documents: 10th and 12th marksheets, CET scorecard, transfer certificate, Aadhaar copy and 6 passport photos.

FEES
Annual tuition fee is 85,000 rupees per year for all B.Tech branches.
Hostel fee is 45,000 rupees per year including mess charges.
The last date to pay fees without a fine is 15th August 2026.
Students can pay fees online through the college portal or at the accounts office.
Scholarships are available for students with family income below 2.5 lakh rupees per year.

LIBRARY
The library is open Monday to Saturday from 8 AM to 8 PM.
Students can borrow 4 books at a time for 14 days.
A fine of 2 rupees per day is charged for late returns.
The digital library has 200+ online journals accessible from the campus network.

HOSTEL
Hostel rooms are on twin sharing basis with WiFi included.
Hostel in-time is 9:30 PM on weekdays and 10:30 PM on weekends.
Hostel warden: Mrs. Sharma, office room H-101.
Hostel leave applications must be approved by the warden one day in advance.

EXAMS AND ACADEMICS
Internal exams are held twice per semester; each carries 20 marks.
Minimum 75% attendance is required to be eligible for end-semester exams.
End semester exams start in the first week of December for odd semester.
Revaluation applications must be submitted within 10 days of results.
